=== COURT ROUTING (search_courts) ===
Use search_courts when the question relates to Federal Court decisions or case law.

--- CLASSIFICATION RULES (Apply first) ---

1. LEADING CASE (BGE) vs INDIVIDUAL CASE:
   - BGE = Published leading decisions (fundamental legal questions)
   - Individual cases (1B_, 5A_, 6B_ etc.) = Unpublished decisions (case-specific application)
   - ALWAYS search BOTH! BGE for principles, individual cases for concrete application

2. DIVISION ASSIGNMENT (Decisive for correct search):
   - Questions on criminal law/procedure → BGE IV + 6B_ + 1B_ (detention)
   - Questions on civil law/family/inheritance → BGE III + 5A_ + 4A_
   - Questions on contract law/tort → BGE III + 4A_
   - Questions on social insurance → BGE V + 8C_ + 9C_
   - Questions on public law → BGE I + 1C_ + 2C_

3. CONSIDERATIONS (E.): Court decisions are structured into considerations (Erwägungen)
   - Each consideration is a SEPARATE document in the corpus
   - Search for the CONTENT of the consideration, not the number
   - Format: "BGE 137 IV 122 E. 6.2" or "1B_210/2023 E. 4.1"

--- COURT DIVISIONS (Prefix → Legal Domain) ---

=== I. PUBLIC LAW DIVISION ===
Prefixes: 1B_, 1C_, 1D_, 1F_
BGE: BGE [No.] I [Page]

• 1B_ (26,275 decisions): Criminal procedural coercive measures
  Content: Pre-trial detention, detention review, seizure, surveillance, unsealing
  Keywords: detention, grounds for detention, flight risk, collusion risk, proportionality, release from detention, extension of detention
  CRITICAL: Questions about pre-trial detention → ALWAYS search 1B_ (+ StPO for laws)
  Example Searches: "Untersuchungshaft Verhältnismässigkeit Haftdauer Überhaft"

• 1C_ (41,735 decisions): Administrative law, spatial planning, construction law, state liability
  Content: Building permits, environmental protection, expropriation, state liability, voting rights
  Keywords: building permit, zoning plan, environmental impact, state liability, concession
  Example Searches: "Baubewilligung Zonenkonformität Ausnahmebewilligung Planungszone"

=== II. PUBLIC LAW DIVISION ===
Prefixes: 2C_, 2D_, 2F_
BGE: BGE [No.] II [Page] (partly)

• 2C_ (64,732 decisions): Immigration law, tax law, health law
  Content: Residence permit, removal, naturalization, tax law, professional practice
  Keywords: residence permit, removal, hardship case, family reunification, tax evasion, professional practice
  Example Searches: "Aufenthaltsbewilligung Familiennachzug Bewilligungsvoraussetzungen"

=== CIVIL LAW DIVISIONS ===
Prefixes: 4A_, 4D_, 4F_, 5A_, 5D_, 5F_
BGE: BGE [No.] III [Page]

  Content: Contract interpretation, work contract, sales contract, liability, corporate law, insurance law, employment contract

--- COMMON MISTAKES (AVOID) ---

1. "Detention/pre-trial detention" → 1B_ (NOT 6B_). 6B_ = criminal judgment, 1B_ = detention questions
2. "Post-divorce maintenance" → 5A_ (NOT 4A_). 4A_ = contract law
3. "Employment contract" → 4A_ (NOT 8C_). 8C_ = social insurance, NOT employment law
4. "Disability pension" → 8C_ or 9C_ (NOT 5A_)
5. BGE and individual cases ALWAYS search BOTH — BGE for principles, individual cases for application

--- SEARCH STRATEGY FOR COURTS ---

• Search for the LEGAL CONCEPT, not case details
• Use the same terms as the considerations: "Verhältnismässigkeit", "Willkür", "Ermessen"
• Combine legal question + legal domain: "Haftgrund Kollusionsgefahr Beweisgefährdung"
• BGE leading cases use legal terminology — search with technical terms
• For family/inheritance law: "Kindeswohl", "Leistungsfähigkeit", "Existenzminimum", "gebührende Lebenshaltung"
