=== LAW ROUTING (search_laws) ===
Use search_laws when the question relates to statutory articles, ordinances, or the Federal Constitution.

--- CLASSIFICATION RULES (Apply first) ---

1. CRIMINAL LAW vs CRIMINAL PROCEDURE:
   - Substantive offense (murder, fraud, theft, bodily harm) → StGB
   - Juvenile offenses (minors, educational measures) → JStG
   - Procedure (pre-trial detention, appeal, evidence) → StPO
   - Juvenile criminal procedure → JStPO
   - TRAP: "Pre-trial detention" (Untersuchungshaft) is ALWAYS StPO (Art. 212-240), NOT StGB

2. PRIVATE LAW vs PROCEDURAL LAW:
   - Substantive claims (contract, property, family, inheritance) → OR/ZGB
   - Property law, land register → ZGB + GBV (Land Register Ordinance)
   - Procedural law (action, appeal, evidence, jurisdiction) → ZPO
   - Private international law → IPRG
   - TRAP: "Divorce" involves ZGB (Art. 111-134) AND ZPO (procedural rules)

3. FEDERAL COURT PROCEDURE:
   - Appeal to Federal Court, standing, deadlines → BGG
   - Federal administrative procedure → VwVG
   - ALWAYS search BGG when "appeal in civil/criminal/public law matters" mentioned
   - Art. 100 para. 1 BGG (30-day deadline) appears in ALMOST EVERY case

4. FINANCIAL MARKETS:
   - Financial services, investment advice, prospectus → FIDLEG
   - Financial institutions, licensing → FINIG
   - Financial market supervision, market abuse → FINMAG
   - Banks → BankG
   - Book-entry securities → BEG
   - FINMA ordinances → FINMA (collective term)

5. MANDATORY MULTI-SEARCH: Most questions involve 2-3 statutes simultaneously.
   Example: Criminal procedure → StPO + StGB + BGG
   Example: Child maintenance → ZGB + ZPO + BGG
   Example: Asylum → AsylG + AIG + BV

--- COMMON MISTAKES (AVOID) ---

1. "Detention" without context → StPO (NOT StGB). StGB governs penalties, StPO governs pre-trial/safety detention
2. "Maintenance" → ZGB Art. 276ff (child maintenance) or Art. 125ff (post-marital maintenance)
3. "Appeal" → BGG (Federal Court), OR StPO Art. 393ff (criminal proceedings), OR ZPO (civil proceedings), OR VwVG (administrative)
4. "Contract" → OR (Code of Obligations), NOT ZGB
5. "Damages" → OR Art. 41ff (tort) OR Art. 97ff (contractual)
6. "Limitation period" → OR Art. 127ff (obligations) OR StGB Art. 97ff (criminal law)
7. "Right to be heard" → BV Art. 29 para. 2 (fundamental right) + respective procedural law
8. "Juvenile" → JStG/JStPO (NOT StGB/StPO!)
9. "Asylum/refugee" → AsylG (NOT AIG, although residence may be involved)
10. "Land register" → GBV + ZGB (Property Law), NOT just ZGB
11. "Financial service" → FIDLEG (NOT BankG or FINMAG)
12. "Data protection" → DSG, NOT BV Art. 13

--- SEARCH STRATEGY FOR LAWS ---

• Use GERMAN legal terms (corpus is in German)
• Combine legal domain + specific legal question: "Kindesunterhalt Berechnung Bedarf Existenzminimum"
• Search BROADLY (different aspects of same question): first substantive, then procedural
• Do NOT use article numbers in search — the embedding model searches semantically
• Use MULTIPLE filter_codes for related areas: e.g. ["StGB", "StPO"] together
