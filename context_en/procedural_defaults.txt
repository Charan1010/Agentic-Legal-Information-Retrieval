=== PROCEDURAL STANDARD CITATIONS (Procedural Defaults) ===

This document lists provisions that are cited in almost EVERY Federal Court case,
REGARDLESS of the substantive legal area. A purely semantic approach will NOT find these,
because they have no substantive connection to the specific legal question — but they are
ALWAYS part of the Gold Citations.

--- STRATEGY ---

After completing all thematic search directions:
1. Determine the case type (criminal, civil, etc.)
2. Add the standard citations for that case type
3. Verify that the citations exist in the corpus (exact string match!)

--- APPLICATION RULES FOR THE AGENT ---

1. AFTER every thematic search: Determine the case type from found citations
2. Add the relevant standard citations from the lists above
3. BUT: Only add if the EXACT string exists in the corpus!
   → "Art. 42 Abs. 2 BGG" must appear as a citation string in laws_de.csv
   → Otherwise DO NOT add (false positive)
4. For DETENTION cases (1B_ prefix found): Mandatory check StPO detention articles + BV Art. 10/31
5. For MIXED cases: Combine multiple case-type lists

--- WHY THESE ARE NOT FOUND SEMANTICALLY ---

Example: Question about child maintenance
  → Semantic search finds: ZGB Art. 276-293, BGE 147 III 265 (maintenance)
  → NOT found: Art. 42 Abs. 2 BGG, Art. 95 BGG, Art. 100 BGG
  → BUT: These are Gold Citations because the Federal Court in its decision
    E. 1 (procedural requirements) ALWAYS cites these provisions

The "standard citations" are the FRAME of every decision.
The content varies, but the frame is constant.
