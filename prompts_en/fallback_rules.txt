--- QUERY GENERATION FOR FALLBACK ---

For each direction without explicit seed_queries:
1. Extract nouns/concepts from the English query
2. Translate using terminology_bridge.txt
3. Combine 3-5 German legal terms into a query
4. Generate 2 variants (different aspects)

Example:
  English: "What are the requirements for extending pre-trial detention?"
  Keywords matched: detention, pre-trial → CRIMINAL PROCEDURE/DETENTION
  Seed queries generated:
    - "Haftverlängerung Voraussetzungen Verhältnismässigkeit Dauer"
    - "Untersuchungshaft Verlängerung Haftgründe Zwangsmassnahmengericht"
